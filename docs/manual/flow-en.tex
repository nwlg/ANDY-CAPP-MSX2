\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{DiHalt Studio logo.} About 4 seconds. It cannot be skipped.
\item \textbf{Cover.} The original box art on the whole screen. It stays until you press \textbf{any key} or move or press the \textbf{joystick} in either port.
\item \textbf{Title.} After a couple of seconds of black screen while the game loads, Andy's front room with the sign \texttt{ANDY CAPP / PRESS FIRE TO START / MSX2 BY DIHALT 2026}. The music starts (Dvo\v{r}\'ak's New World Symphony) while the alcometer fills up. Press \key{SPACE} or the joystick button to start.
\item \textbf{The game.} Monday, 08:00, in the front room at home, with \pounds 9.50 and 30 kisses. The music keeps playing during the game.
\end{enumerate}

When the game ends (won or lost), you are back at the title screen to start another one.
