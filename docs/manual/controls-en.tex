\begin{itemize}
\item To enter a building or turn a corner, \textbf{face} the door or the corner and press up: Andy turns his back and goes in.
\item The joystick works in \textbf{port 1 and port 2}.
\item A message stays on the screen until you press \key{SPACE} or the button, so you have time to read it. The clock keeps running meanwhile, and the time on the panel keeps up with it.
\item There is no pause key.
\end{itemize}
